Badge slots

Key: Attributes & badges

Attributes (Build tab): You have a 2,650-point budget to distribute across your player ratings, excluding Durability. Durability remains available as a stat for injury-story roleplay, but changing it does not use or refund build points. Raising or lowering other attributes changes your build and can change which badges you qualify for. You do not have to spend every point.

Badge eligibility: Each badge has attribute thresholds. Your relevant rating must meet the requirement to select that badge; having an available slot alone does not make you eligible. Badge requirements are checked from your current attributes.

Badge tiers: Bronze, Silver and Gold are available during initial creation. Hall of Fame and Legend are unlocked later through fixed-cost upgrades. The tier you can equip depends on the badge requirements and your attributes. Height limits are not applied in this creator.

Slots and trades: Start with 3 Gold, 9 Silver and 8 Bronze slots. Trade 1 Gold slot for +2 Silver slots, or 1 Silver slot for +2 Bronze slots. Trades lower the upper-tier slot limit and increase the tier below; the total number of slots can therefore increase. If you reduce a tier’s limit, badges over that limit are unequipped.

Quick example: Trade 1 Gold and you have 2 Gold slots and 11 Silver slots. Trade 1 Silver as well and you have 2 Gold, 10 Silver and 10 Bronze slots.

20 base badge slots: 3 Gold, 9 Silver and 8 Bronze. Trade higher-tier slots down to unlock more lower-tier slots. Only badges your attributes qualify for are selectable.

A traded slot reduces the upper-tier limit and adds extra slots to the tier below. You can change these trades at any time; equipped badges over the new limit will be unequipped.

Structure a swap like the 2K trade screen: choose two teams, then up to five players per side. Each selected player moves to the opposite team when you apply. Empty slots are ignored.

4 · OCR · Update roster from screenshot

Upload a 2K roster, player-card or team screenshot. OCR reads visible text, matches names against your existing roster, and proposes changes for review before saving. Clear, uncropped screenshots work best.

OCR runs in your browser. The first use downloads the OCR engine and needs an internet connection.

5 · OCR · Import rookie draft

Upload a screenshot of the NBA 2K rookie draft board or draft results. OCR proposes rookies and their teams, positions and OVRs. Review the editable rows before adding or updating them in your stored roster database.

Use a clear screenshot where player names and team names are readable.

6 · OCR · League trades / transactions

Upload the MyNBA screen that lists league-wide trades or transactions. The app matches visible player names and suggests destination teams. Review or correct each destination before applying; accepted moves immediately update the stored rosters on the Rosters page.

Each player move is reviewed before any roster is changed.

7 · Backup / move roster (JSON)

Export a full roster backup on one device, then import that JSON file on another. This is a manual transfer; automatic cross-device sync requires a shared cloud backend and account setup.
